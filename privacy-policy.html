PushBoss Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Short version:

  • PushBoss has no accounts, no sign-in, and no user database. We never ask for your email.
  • Camera video is analysed entirely on your phone to count reps. Frames are never uploaded, never stored by the rep counter, and never seen by us.
  • Optional session recordings (including microphone audio, if you allow it) stay on your device and in your Photos library. They are only shared if you choose to share them.
  • We receive anonymous product analytics (PostHog) and anonymous subscription data (RevenueCat). No advertising, no ad networks, no cross-app tracking, no IDFA.
  • Deleting the app deletes all of your PushBoss data from your device.

1. Who We Are

PushBoss (the "App") is developed and operated by Aleksandr Prikhodko, an individual developer trading as "PushBoss" (referred to as "we", "us", or "our"). For data protection purposes, we are the data controller for the limited information described below.

Contact: pushboss.app@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, where that information lives, and what rights you have. It applies to the PushBoss mobile application only.

2. No Accounts, No Sign-In

PushBoss does not offer account creation and does not support sign-in of any kind. There is no "Sign in with Apple", no Google sign-in, no email or password, and no social login. We do not collect your email address, phone number, postal address, or payment details.

Because there is no account, there is nothing for us to log you into and no user profile held on our side. See Section 8 for how deletion works in practice.

3. Information Stored On Your Device

Almost everything PushBoss knows about you is stored locally on your phone and never leaves it. This includes:

  • The first name you type during setup. It is used only to address you inside the App. It is stored on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • Your setup answers that we keep: fitness level, weekly workout goal, fitness goal, your self-reported max-pushups range, and your chosen content pack/art style.
  • Workout history and progress: completed sessions, rep counts, session duration, streaks, streak freezes, badges, challenges, and experience points.
  • App settings: music on/off, session recording on/off, tutorial and onboarding completion flags, subscription status flags, and your referral code and referral counts.

Setup answers we deliberately discard: the gender question and the "how often do you think about getting in shape" question are used only to pick the tone of the next screen. Those answers are held in temporary memory, are never written to storage, and are never transmitted anywhere.

4. Camera, Microphone, and Recordings

Camera and pose detection

To count your pushups, the App runs on-device pose detection (Google ML Kit Pose Detection) over the live camera feed. Camera frames are processed in real time on your device, are not written to disk by the rep counter, are never uploaded to any server, and are never accessible to us or to any third party. No image, video frame, or body-landmark data is transmitted off your phone.

Optional session recording

PushBoss offers an optional screen recording of your workout session, which you can turn on or off in Settings. When it is enabled:

  • The recording captures your session screen, including the camera view. If you grant microphone permission, the recording also captures audio from your microphone, so that your own sound is part of the clip.
  • The resulting video file is stored on your device and is automatically saved to a "PushBoss" album in your Photos library.
  • Recordings are never uploaded to us. They leave your device only if you personally choose to share them, for example to TikTok or via the system share sheet, in which case the receiving app's own privacy policy applies.

Photo library

The App requests permission to add media to your photo library so it can save your session recordings. It does not read, browse, or upload your existing photos.

5. Information Sent To Third Parties

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers. The App communicates with a small number of service providers, listed in full below. Each of these providers is bound by its own contractual and legal obligations to protect the data it receives.

Service Purpose What it receives
PostHog (PostHog, Inc., US cloud — us.i.posthog.com) Anonymous product analytics, so we can see which parts of the App are used and where people get stuck. A randomly generated anonymous installation identifier, plus events such as screens viewed, buttons tapped, sessions completed, rep counts, session duration, selected fitness level and weekly frequency, and basic device and app version information. It does not receive your name, email, camera data, recordings, or any advertising identifier. Automatic event capture and session replay are disabled.
RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) Managing subscription status and restoring purchases across devices. An anonymous RevenueCat identifier, your subscription and transaction status from the App Store, and the anonymous analytics identifier described above (so the two anonymous records can be matched). No name and no email.
Apple (App Store / StoreKit) Processing purchases, subscriptions, trials, and refunds. Apple handles the entire payment flow. We never see or receive your payment card, billing address, or Apple Account credentials. Apple's own privacy policy governs that processing.
Expo (Expo EAS Update, 650 Industries, Inc.) Delivering over-the-air bug fixes and updates to the App. Standard technical request information when the App checks for an update, such as app version, platform, and the IP address of the request.

We may also disclose information where we are legally required to do so, or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. If the App is ever transferred to another owner, the limited anonymous data described above may transfer with it, and this policy would be updated first.

6. What The App Does Not Do

  • No push notifications. The App does not send push notifications and does not request notification permission.
  • No advertising and no cross-app tracking. There are no ad networks, no IDFA/advertising identifier access, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because the App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No health-app integration. The App does not read from or write to Apple Health, Google Fit, or any similar health platform, and it does not collect your weight, height, age, or medical information.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information as those terms are defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act. We have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding twelve months.
  • No user database. We operate no server that stores records about individual users.

7. Legal Bases and International Transfers

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our legal bases for processing are: performance of a contract with you, for delivering the App's functionality and your subscription; and our legitimate interests, for anonymous analytics used to keep the App working, fix problems, and improve it. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests at any time using the contact address below.

The service providers listed in Section 5 are located in the United States, so the limited anonymous data they receive is processed there. Those transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses and equivalent safeguards.

8. Retention and Deletion

  • On-device data is kept for as long as the App is installed. Deleting PushBoss from your device permanently deletes all of it — your name, setup answers, history, streaks, and settings. This is immediate and requires no request to us. Session recordings already saved to your Photos library are yours and are not removed by uninstalling; you can delete them in the Photos app.
  • Anonymous analytics events are retained by PostHog on a rolling basis for as long as they remain useful for product analysis, and no longer than 24 months.
  • Subscription records are retained by RevenueCat and Apple for as long as required for billing, tax, and accounting purposes.

Because there is no account, Apple's in-app account deletion requirement does not apply. If you nevertheless want the anonymous analytics record for your installation erased, email us at pushboss.app@gmail.com and we will delete it. Please note that we cannot look up an anonymous record without the installation identifier, so the simplest and most complete option remains deleting the App.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the information we hold about you, and receive a copy of it
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete information
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact pushboss.app@gmail.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. In most cases you can exercise the access and deletion rights yourself and instantly, because the data is on your own device.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

The App's design is its main security property: sensitive material — camera frames, recordings, and your name — stays on your device and is never transmitted. Data in transit to the service providers listed above is encrypted using HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
